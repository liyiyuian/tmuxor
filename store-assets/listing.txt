DESCRIPTION
-----------
G2C — your Herdr fleet of Claude Code and Codex sessions, hands-free on your glasses.

G2C is a heads-up conductor for developers who run many coding-agent sessions at once. It brings the Claude Code and Codex sessions running in Herdr (herdr.dev) onto your Even G2 glasses, so you can stay in flow without reaching for the laptop.

• Your layout — sessions in Herdr's own order (workspaces, then tabs); pinned ones first, finished ones brought up until you open them. Choose which workspaces to show.
• Status at a glance — idle, working, needs you, finished — plus how much of your Claude and Codex plan limits is left, in the top row.
• Preview before you open — the highlighted session's last request and the end of its reply sit beside the list.
• Real conversations — the actual prompts and replies, not terminal noise, including prompts still waiting in the agent's queue. Reopen where you left off.
• Voice replies — tap, speak (keep reading while it listens), review the transcription and its cost, send.
• Approve safely — read the full command before you pick an option.
• Glasses menu (tap, then hold) — jump to the next session that needs you, switch a reply between condensed and full, pin a session, and flip the preview, AI summary, plan usage, wake-on-finish and screen sleep without your phone.
• Optional AI help, with your own keys — a short summary in the preview and a one-screen version of long replies. Off by default; you choose the model (Claude Haiku by default) and see what your API calls cost.
• Start new sessions — pick a project tag, speak a folder, and Claude opens there.
• Private by design — talks only to your own backend on your own machine, over Tailscale, behind your token. Nothing is sent to the app developer.

Requires the Even Realities app 2.2.10 or later, and a one-time backend setup on your computer (Herdr + Claude Code + Tailscale). Setup guide: github.com/liyiyuian/tmuxor — run install.sh, then paste the printed code into the app.

PERMISSIONS (justifications)
----------------------------
network — Connects to your own G2C backend over Tailscale to read and drive
your Herdr sessions. (Whitelisted to Tailscale hosts; the app talks only
to the backend URL you enter in Setup.)

g2-microphone — Captures glasses microphone audio for voice replies, transcribed
by your configured backend.


PRIVACY POLICY
--------------
G2C collects no personal data and sends nothing to its developer. It connects
only to the backend URL you configure — running on your own device, reached over
your private Tailscale network — using an access token you provide. Microphone
audio you record is sent to your configured backend, which transcribes it (by
default via OpenAI's Whisper API using your own API key) and does not retain it.
AI summaries and condensed replies are off by default; if you turn one on, your
backend sends the session's latest request and reply to Anthropic using your own
API key, with the model you choose. Plan-usage figures are read by your backend
from files on your own computer and shown only to you.
Your backend URL, access token, and preferences are stored locally on your phone
only. The app contains no analytics, ads, or third-party tracking. You are
responsible for securing your backend and API keys.
